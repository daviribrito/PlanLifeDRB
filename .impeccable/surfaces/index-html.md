## Scope
Whole app shell (index.html) and all routes: Hoje, Rotina, Ciclos, Agenda, Finanças, Cofre, Assistente, Ajustes. Mode: Operate. Single user, desktop first.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The owner's life is planned like an athlete's season: a fixed base routine plus periodized focus cycles (month / week / days). Refuses the neutral SaaS card dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Cool white track-paper ground, navy ink. Each focus mode owns one saturated band hue (Saúde vermilion, Inglês blue, Estudos violet, Finanças green). The side rail is tinted by today's active cycle. Barlow for UI, Barlow Condensed for numerals and headings, tabular figures everywhere.
STORY: Opens on today: what cycle I'm in, my day laid out by real duration, free time left, and AI extras to accept or reject. Everything else one click away in the rail.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Rail left (tinted). Header guide line names date, ISO week, active cycle and day N of M. Under it the periodization strip (signature move): six weeks of days in three stacked bands (Mês / Semana / Dias) with a today marker. Below: left the duration-proportional day timeline, right the cycle suggestions and AI questions with Aceitar / Recusar.
FORM: Training periodization chart, my top-ranked grounded candidate (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, user chosen). Seed key b2c402ab. Raises: state by line form, not hue (from emission-line rail): solid done, dashed pending, struck skipped. Day as time budget with free time shown (from cassette j-card). Block length equals exact duration (from labanotation). Header guide words naming the span in view (from lexicon).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
